\section*{Limitations}

This is an observational case study of one OLMo-2-7B prune--regrow recipe.
keep14, ShortGPT, and the same-shape points are single runs; item-level
intervals do not measure variation from initialization, data order,
optimization, or block selection. Shallower arms stop at unequal retained
checkpoints while PPL is still falling, and full32 stops at 25k. There was no
registered common stopping rule, so neither the depth ladder nor full32 is a
step-, token-, or FLOP-matched 200k counterfactual.

Random-init uses a higher learning rate, frozen-front changes the trainable
set, and ShortGPT changes inherited count, selected layers, final-block
retention, and fresh-tail use. These are operating points, not factor-isolating
ablations. The two MMLU protocols also change prompt, candidate, tokenization,
and normalization; closed-book scores add generation and normalization choices.
Only selected answer-letter contrasts have paired intervals.

The study covers English base models, one mixture, and mainly 7B scale. PPL is
in-domain; no contamination audit or out-of-domain PPL is reported. We also do
not report latency, throughput, memory, or recovery FLOPs. Exact reproduction
is limited by unset training seeds, an unrecorded resumed data-loader offset,
incomplete compute accounting, and no frozen runnable artifact. Layer-wise
readouts are relegated to Appendix~\ref{app:mechanism} and carry no mechanistic
claim.
