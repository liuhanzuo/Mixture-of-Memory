\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This OLMo case study records a recovery path rather than proposing a pruning
method. In the single principal prefix run, in-domain likelihood improves
substantially while answer-letter MMLU and closed-book QA remain far below the
intact model. An available short-horizon full32 branch, an exploratory MMLU
interface comparison, same-shape operating points, and a coupled ShortGPT
construction comparison narrow the interpretation without isolating corpus,
initialization, adaptation, or structural causes. Perplexity remains essential
for distributional fit, but within these observed interventions it is not a
sufficient one-number summary of target-behavior recovery. The practical
proposal is to report likelihood, capability, interface, exact construction,
and recovery budget separately, with seed and matching limitations explicit.
