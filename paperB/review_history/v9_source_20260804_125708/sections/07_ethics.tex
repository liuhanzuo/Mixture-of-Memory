\section*{Ethical Considerations}

This work adds no safety mechanism and inherits risks of the underlying model
and data, including hallucination, bias, and unsafe generation. Its practical
caution is measurement-related: better in-domain perplexity need not imply
recovery of capabilities required in deployment, so target evaluations and
relevant null baselines should be checked directly.

We collect no human-subject data or annotations. Experiments use released
models, corpora, and benchmarks under their upstream terms. Training and
large-scale evaluation consume energy; incomplete historical records prevent a
reliable GPU-hour total. Released artifacts should preserve upstream licenses
and exclude benchmark text or model weights that cannot be redistributed.
