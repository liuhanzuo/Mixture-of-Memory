\section{A Multi-Axis Recovery Audit}
\label{sec:observation}

Let $P_t$ denote in-domain perplexity and $E_t$ a knowledge-sensitive
evaluation at checkpoint $t$. A common informal recovery argument is
\[
P_{t_2}<P_{t_1}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{target recovery at }t_2 .
\]
Here ``target recovery'' means closing the large deficit to the intact-base
score on the same evaluation, not merely obtaining a statistically detectable
within-run gain. We audit that argument on three axes:
\textbf{trajectory} (does the target gap close as PPL falls?),
\textbf{interface} (does the verdict survive a change in scoring, and what is
the null floor?), and \textbf{construction} (do models with the same nominal
depth reach similar endpoints?). The full32 branch adds an intact
same-corpus reference. This design yields diagnostic operating-point
comparisons rather than a single scalar recovery score.
