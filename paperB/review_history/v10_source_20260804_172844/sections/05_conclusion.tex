\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Our OLMo-2-7B audit shows that keep14 improves yet retains a 28.6-point MMLU
deficit; its content-interface gain is smaller than the random-null gain; and
the gap recurs in closed-book QA. At the same depth and displayed steps,
ShortGPT is 15.5 letter-MMLU points stronger and improves all three closed-book
tasks, while coupling four construction factors. The likelihood ordering also
transfers to WikiText-103 and PG-19, and measured continuation-stream overlap
does not explain the gaps. Recovery claims should therefore report likelihood,
target behavior, interface, construction, budget, and uncertainty together.
