\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

On the literal observed OLMo paths, in-domain PPL improvement alone does not
imply recovery to intact-base performance on the measured knowledge-sensitive
evaluations. The random content baseline, the 25k-only full32 branch, and the
coupled ShortGPT construction bound several complete explanations while leaving
their confounds explicit. The supported contribution is a proxy-validity case
study and a reporting discipline: likelihood, target evaluation, interface,
construction, budget/compute, and run-level uncertainty should be stated
separately. We did not test a prospective PPL threshold and do not infer
knowledge deletion or location, causal structural factors, universal recovery
dynamics, or failure beyond the observed budgets.
