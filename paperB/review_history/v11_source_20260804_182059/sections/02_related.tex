\paragraph{Selecting and removing depth.}
ShortGPT and SLEB rank and remove redundant blocks
~\citep{men2024shortgpt,song2024sleb}; LaCo merges adjacent layers
~\citep{yang2024laco}; BlockPruner searches at attention/MLP-block granularity
~\citep{blockpruner}. Other studies show that preferred removals depend on task
and calibration choices~\citep{siddiqui2024deeper,lu2024reassessing,
calibration2026}. These works optimize \emph{which} structure to retain. We
instead measure what different retained structures recover under CPT.

\paragraph{Recovery paths and retraining controls.}
\citet{gromov2024unreasonable} are the closest antecedent for post-healing
loss--task dissociation after removing deeper layers. Shortened LLaMA reports
CPT learning curves and compares CPT, LoRA, and pruned versus scratch
initialization~\citep{shortenedllama}. Minitron likewise studies retraining
trajectories, iterative pruning, task behavior, and initialization choices
within a structured-pruning/distillation pipeline~\citep{minitron}.
IteRABRe explicitly alternates block removal and recovery, plotting task-family
trajectories and weak MMLU recovery~\citep{iterabre}. Thus neither trajectories
nor loss--task dissociation originate here. What these studies do not jointly
provide is a same-item interface audit, a random interface floor, closed-book
factual generation, an intact same-corpus CPT reference, and a same-depth
construction contrast. We assemble those pieces into one auditable recovery
study. PASER is complementary: it selects post-training data to improve
recovery efficiency rather than auditing the validity of recovery
measurements~\citep{paser}.
Sheared LLaMA, compact-model distillation, and DRPruning integrate pruning with
data allocation, distillation, or robust training~\citep{xia2024sheared,
muralidharan2024compact,deng2025drpruning}. Our prefix-plus-fresh-tail recipe is
not presented as competitive with these recovery algorithms.

\paragraph{Repair and concurrent work.}
LinearPatch and Prune\&Comp attribute part of layer-pruning damage to
cross-layer interface or magnitude mismatch and repair it with lightweight
transformations or compensation~\citep{linearpatch,prunecomp}. A decision-
transition analysis studies where post-pruning performance collapse appears
~\citep{decisioncollapse}. SlimQwen and ShortOPD appeared within three months of
this manuscript version and are treated as concurrent work
~\citep{slimqwen,shortopd}; they further cover matched-token initialization,
progressive recovery, and recognition/generation behavior. ShortOPD and
concurrent work on generative reasoning limits strengthen the case for
evaluating free generation alongside multiple-choice recognition
~\citep{limitsgenerativereasoning}. Our study complements these recovery
methods by asking which measurements justify the word ``recovered.''

\paragraph{Evaluating compression beyond perplexity.}
The Cost of Compression studies how pruning and quantization affect parametric
knowledge~\citep{costcompression}. \citet{jaiswal2024truth} show that low
perplexity can coexist with large deficits on knowledge-intensive compressed-LM
benchmarks, and \citet{beyondperplexity} document divergent safety effects.
These studies motivate multi-dimensional evaluation. Our additional focus is
measurement validity during post-depth-pruning CPT: within-arm checkpoints, an
available intact same-corpus branch, a random null floor, paired scoring
interfaces, closed-book generation, and an alternative same-depth
construction.
Fragile Knowledge, Robust Instruction-Following reports selective capability
effects under width pruning~\citep{fragileknowledge}; it is adjacent evidence
that compression can preserve some interfaces while damaging others.
Multiple-choice evaluations are themselves interface-sensitive: first-token
letter probabilities can disagree with generated text, leaderboard rankings
change with evaluation details, and MMLU varies under answer reordering
~\citep{myanswerisc,benchmarktargets,answerorder}. Our content protocol is a
diagnostic within this broader literature. Its key addition is the random
same-shape floor, which shows that a larger complete-option score is not by
itself evidence of inherited target recovery.
