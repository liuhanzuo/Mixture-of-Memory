\section*{Limitations}

This is a measurement study of one principal OLMo-2-7B pruning recipe, not a new
state-of-the-art compression method. keep14 and the same-shape controls are
single training runs; item-level intervals quantify evaluation uncertainty, not
training-seed variance. Shallower prefix arms stop at retained plateau
checkpoints rather than a common 200k step, and the intact full32 control stops
at 25k. Consequently, the depth ladder and full32 comparison are not
step-, token-exposure-, or FLOP-matched long-budget counterfactuals.

The fully random model uses a higher learning rate, while frozen-front changes
the trainable parameter set. They are informative operating points rather than
clean initialization or adaptation ablations. ShortGPT differs from keep14 in
inherited block count, non-contiguous selection, final-block retention, and the
absence of a fresh tail; its advantage cannot
be attributed to any one of those factors. It shows only that the two coupled
constructions yield different endpoints.

Answer-letter and complete-option MMLU use different prompts and candidate
scoring. Their gap is consistent with an interface component, but the
random-init fluency floor prevents content accuracy from being interpreted as
subject knowledge. Conversely, closed-book exact match depends on generation
and normalization. We therefore rely on agreement across interfaces and
datasets rather than designating one metric as ground truth.

Layer-wise logit-lens and linear-probe thresholds are readout dependent. Their
ordering is descriptive and neither localizes factual storage nor proves that
removed layers contained the missing capability. The study covers English base
models, one healing mixture, and primarily 7B scale; instruction tuning, other
architectures, pruning policies, corpora, and longer budgets may produce
different recovery trajectories.

Held-out PPL is measured on a disjoint shard from the same Dolmino/DCLM source
as healing. We do not report out-of-domain PPL or a benchmark-contamination
audit, so the likelihood axis should be interpreted as in-domain distributional
fit and the knowledge-sensitive results as observed benchmark behavior, not a
clean estimate of newly recovered world knowledge.
