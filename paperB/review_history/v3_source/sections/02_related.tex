\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

We do not propose a new pruning criterion. Our object of study is the recovery
trajectory after a structural intervention and the controls needed to interpret
it. Table~\ref{tab:priorart} gives a compact positioning.

\paragraph{Selecting and removing depth.}
ShortGPT and SLEB rank and remove redundant blocks
~\citep{men2024shortgpt,song2024sleb}; LaCo merges adjacent layers
~\citep{yang2024laco}; BlockPruner searches at attention/MLP-block granularity
~\citep{blockpruner}. Other studies show that preferred removals depend on task
and calibration choices~\citep{siddiqui2024deeper,lu2024reassessing,
calibration2026}. These works optimize \emph{which} structure to retain. We
instead measure what different retained structures recover under CPT.

\paragraph{Retraining pruned models.}
\citet{gromov2024unreasonable} remove contiguous deeper layers and show that
post-healing autoregressive loss and downstream tasks can behave differently.
Our narrower increment is to treat the recovery path itself as the analysis
object and add same-corpus, same-shape, scoring-interface, construction, and
closed-book controls. Shortened LLaMA directly
compares CPT and LoRA after depth pruning~\citep{shortenedllama}. Sheared LLaMA,
Minitron, compact-model distillation, and DRPruning combine pruning with data
allocation or teacher supervision~\citep{xia2024sheared,minitron,
muralidharan2024compact,deng2025drpruning}. Our prefix-plus-fresh-tail recipe is
not proposed as a superior compression algorithm; it is a controlled setting in
which to track recovery and contrast structural policies.

\paragraph{Evaluating compression beyond perplexity.}
The Cost of Compression studies how pruning and quantization affect parametric
knowledge~\citep{costcompression}. \citet{jaiswal2024truth} show that low
perplexity can coexist with large deficits on knowledge-intensive compressed-LM
benchmarks, and \citet{beyondperplexity} document divergent safety effects.
These studies motivate multi-dimensional evaluation. Our additional focus is
temporal and causal-inference discipline: within-arm checkpoints, an intact
same-corpus control, same-shape operating points, scoring-interface
decomposition, and a construction comparison.

\paragraph{Readout across depth.}
Logit-lens and tuned-lens analyses decode intermediate predictions
~\citep{nostalgebraist2020logitlens,tunedlens}; feed-forward memory,
knowledge-neuron, and causal-tracing work connect particular computations to
factual behavior~\citep{geva2021transformer,dai2022knowledge,
meng2022locating}. DoLa and LayerSkip exploit differences in layer-wise
predictions for decoding or early exit~\citep{chuang2024dola,layerskip}.
We use readout depth only as a descriptive correlate. Neither a probe
transition nor pruning damage identifies where knowledge is stored.
