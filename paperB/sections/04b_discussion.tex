\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Recovery is multi-axis.}
Perplexity measures the CPT objective, not restoration of a target behavior.
The intact-base gap, OOD likelihood, interface null floor, and closed-book
results jointly show why a loss curve must be interpreted with the target
interface and model construction. Detailed reporting recommendations appear in
Appendix~\ref{app:reporting-checklist}.
