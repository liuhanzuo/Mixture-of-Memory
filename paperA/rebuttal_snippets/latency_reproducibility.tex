\paragraph{Reproducibility of the Read-latency measurement.}
The medians in Table~\ref{tab:replay-latency} come from three independent processes
of $20$ timed reads each over one fixed $\sim$6.5k-token pack (identical
\texttt{sha256}), pooled and reduced by the median: $931.92$\,ms at $j{=}0$ and
$664.36$\,ms at $j{=}12$, with pooled $p10/p90$ of $931.51/941.94$ and
$663.71/667.10$\,ms. To check stability we repeated the measurement on an
otherwise-idle node of the same type (H20, identical torch/CUDA build) using the
same pack, and obtained $936.97$ / $667.53$\,ms: both arms shift by $\approx{+}0.5\%$
together, so the reported ratio reproduces to four significant figures
($1.40365$ versus $1.40274$). The quality side of the same paired cohort is
released as
\texttt{anonymous\_artifact/scores/p0\_13\_quality\_latency/\{summary,stats\}.json},
which also carries the $-3.12$-point RULER gap, its paired-bootstrap
$95\%$ CI $[2.36,3.93]$, the exact McNemar counts, and an
\texttt{all\_packs\_paired\_1to1} assertion.
