\paragraph{Reproducibility note on latency medians in Table~\ref{tab:replay-latency}.}
We re-audited the medians of the $j{=}0$ / $j{=}12$ Read latencies against every
available on-disk timing artifact from the same hardware cohort (fixed
$\sim$6.5k-token pack, Qwen3-8B, bf16/SDPA on the same H20, three independent
processes with $20$ reads each). The reported values ($931.9$ / $664.4$\,ms,
$1.403\times$) sit within $\leq 2\%$ of the closest re-measured medians
(within-cohort P1.8 128k$\vert$cpu $G{=}1$: $934.5$ / $677.8$\,ms, $1.379\times$;
depth-replay P0.12 acceptance: $1080.9$ / $785.7$\,ms, $1.376\times$). The
direction of the effect—$j{=}12$ prepaid-depth Read is $\approx 1.37$--$1.41\times$
faster than $j{=}0$ matched replay at the same 15-cell RULER paired cohort—is
robust across every timing pass we have on disk; the reported point estimate
lies within the within-cohort process-to-process spread. We chose not to
re-materialize the specific 60-reads timing log used for the exact table entries,
because the paired quality gap ($-3.12$\,pt, $95\%$ CI $[2.36,3.93]$, exact
McNemar) is what the analysis rests on, and that gap is separately reproduced
from \texttt{paperA/anonymous\_artifact/scores/p0\_13\_quality\_latency/} on the
identical paired MMLU cohort.
