\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

CoMem makes transformer depth an explicit cross-query reuse axis: contexts are
written to intermediate residuals, a bounded set is selected per query, and only
the upper layers are resumed. The contribution is the measured frontier, not a
claim of free compression or a leaderboard win. With identical evidence and
adapter, $j{=}12$ yields $1.403\times$ faster model Read at a 3.12-point RULER
cost, and its 8\,KiB/token store pays off only after repeated queries. At equal
online latency, raw-text replay remains substantially more accurate. A paired
context--position factorization further localizes one synthetic multikey gap to
the lower-layer Write representation, and overlap recovers most of that local
gap without enlarging the store or Read work. The next decisive step is a
same-backbone, end-to-end comparison with PIC and learned modular-KV systems,
plus natural-task validation of contextual writing. Until then, CoMem should be
read as a transparent depth-reuse design point with a bounded diagnosis.
