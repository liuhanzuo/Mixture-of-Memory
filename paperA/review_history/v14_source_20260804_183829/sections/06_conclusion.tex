\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

CoMem makes prepaid transformer depth a measurable reusable-context coordinate.
On Qwen3-8B, prepaying 12 of 36 layers gives a $1.403\times$ selected-pack Read
speedup at a 3.12-point RULER cost, while the Write-inclusive pipeline reaches
$2.74\times$. Oracle, overlap, and trained-Write diagnostics locate the tested
loss at the Write interface; equal-latency replay exposes its selector-dependent
boundary. Together, the results establish depth as a tunable systems axis
alongside selection budget, stored representation, and placement.
