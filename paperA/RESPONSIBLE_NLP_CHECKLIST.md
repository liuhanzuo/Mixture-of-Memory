# Responsible NLP Checklist Draft — Paper A

This file is for the ARR submission form and is not included in the anonymous
paper PDF.

## A. Scope, limitations, and risks

- **A1 — Yes.** See the dedicated **Limitations** section after the conclusion.
- **A2 — Yes.** See **Ethical Considerations** in the appendix. It discusses
  disclosure from selected source text, inversion/membership-inference risk for
  residual stores, authorization, deletion, isolation, misuse, and energy use.

## B. Scientific artifacts

- **B1 — Yes.** The paper cites the released models, corpora, benchmarks,
  retrieval methods, and software systems used.
- **B2 — Yes, with artifact-specific details in Appendix A.4.** Qwen3 weights
  are used under Apache-2.0; the anonymous code release should use an explicit
  license. Dataset licenses and usage terms are reported where available.
- **B3 — Yes.** All artifacts are used for research evaluation or model
  inference in ways consistent with their documented intended use. The release
  excludes model weights and benchmark text.
- **B4 — No new data were collected.** We do not redistribute benchmark text or
  private conversations. LoCoMo conversations are synthetic, generated by paired
  language-model agents. Potential sensitive-source risks for deployments are
  discussed in Ethical Considerations.
- **B5 — Yes.** Appendix A.4 reports that evaluated language data are English
  and describes benchmark domains and scope.
- **B6 — Yes.** Appendix A.4 and Appendix B give task-level sample counts,
  splits, aggregation, and statistical cohorts.

## C. Computational experiments

- **C1 — Partially yes.** Model parameter counts, GPU types, and the final
  adapter budget (approximately 2.9 H20 GPU-hours) are reported in Appendix A.4.
  A reliable total over all exploratory and failed runs was not consistently
  logged; this omission is disclosed.
- **C2 — Yes.** Appendix A.4 reports the exact backbone revision, optimizer,
  learning rate, warmup, batch, steps, LoRA targets, retrieval parameters, and
  evaluation settings.
- **C3 — Yes.** Tables and captions identify point estimates, paired bootstrap
  intervals, cluster bootstrap intervals, and the seed-plus-effective-batch
  adapter robustness check. Most system ablations are single-run measurements,
  stated in Limitations.
- **C4 — Yes.** Appendix A.4 reports the software stack and version ranges;
  modified evaluation and inference paths are included in the anonymous code
  release.

## D. Human participants

- **D1–D5 — Not applicable.** We recruited no annotators and collected no new
  human-subject data.

## E. AI assistance

- **E1 — Yes.** Generative AI assistants were used for literature search,
  low-novelty drafting and language revision, coding assistance, table/figure
  preparation, and critical review. AutoFigure-Edit and an image-generation
  model were used in the Figure 1 workflow; the final figure is editable and was
  manually reconstructed and checked. Human authors selected the research
  questions, methods, experiments, claims, and citations; they verified every
  reported number and reference and take full responsibility for correctness,
  originality, and potential text/code overlap. No AI system is an author.
